BREAKFAST MENUS

Breakfast Menu Guide

Breakfast menus can include eggs, sandwiches, pancakes, pastries, sides, coffee and other morning favorites.

Common breakfast menu sections

Many restaurants group breakfast into plates, sandwiches, bakery items, sides and drinks. Some locations offer breakfast only during specific morning hours.

What to check

Check serving times, portion descriptions, available sides and drink options. Menu items and availability can vary by restaurant location.

Before you order

Confirm the current breakfast menu and hours with the restaurant, especially on weekends and holidays.
