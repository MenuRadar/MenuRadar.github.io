Restaurant Menu Prices: A Practical Guide

Restaurant menu prices can vary by location, menu format, portion size, taxes, promotions and delivery platform.

What affects menu prices?

A restaurant may use different prices for dine-in, takeaway and delivery. Local operating costs can also affect prices between cities and locations. Specials and limited-time items may have different pricing from the regular menu.

How to compare menu prices

Start by comparing similar portions and meal categories. Check whether drinks, sides or extras are included. When ordering online, review the final checkout total because fees and taxes may be added separately.

Are restaurant menu prices always current?

No. Menus and prices can change without notice. MenuRadar is an independent information site, so readers should confirm important pricing directly with the restaurant before ordering.
