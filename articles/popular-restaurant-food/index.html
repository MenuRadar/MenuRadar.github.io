Popular Restaurant Food Guide

Restaurant menus cover many cuisines and categories, from burgers and pizza to pasta, chicken, sandwiches, desserts and drinks.

Popular menu categories

Common menu categories include appetizers, main dishes, sides, desserts and beverages. Fast-casual and fast-food restaurants may use different category names.

How to choose from a menu

Start with the cuisine and meal type you want, then compare portion size, ingredients, sides and price. If dietary requirements matter, check the restaurant's current ingredient information.

Menu information changes

Restaurants can add seasonal items, remove dishes or change recipes. Confirm current details with the restaurant before ordering.
