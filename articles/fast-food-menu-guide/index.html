FAST FOOD MENUS

Fast Food Menu Guide

Fast food menus often organize choices into burgers, chicken, pizza, sandwiches, sides, desserts and drinks.

Compare complete meals

When comparing fast food options, look at the main item together with sides and drinks rather than comparing the headline price alone.

Look for location differences

Large chains can offer different items, prices and promotions in different markets. Delivery menus may also differ from in-store menus.

Check current availability

Limited-time items and promotions can end. Confirm the current menu with the restaurant before ordering.
